FAQ

Common questions

How does the AI design process work?

You type a text prompt describing your design idea. Our AI model generates 4 unique design options in seconds. You can pick one, request variations, or tweak the prompt until you're happy — then place your order.

What is the print quality like?

We use direct-to-garment (DTG) printing on premium 180 GSM ring-spun cotton. Designs are vibrant, durable, and fade-resistant for up to 50 washes. We also offer screen printing for bulk orders of 50+ shirts.

How long does delivery take?

Mysuru orders: same-day or next-day delivery. Bengaluru: 1–2 days. Rest of India: 3–7 business days. All orders come with a tracking link.

Can I order for a corporate event or college fest?

Absolutely! We specialise in bulk orders for events, startups, colleges, and brands. Contact us for custom quotes, bulk pricing, and dedicated support.

What if I'm not happy with my order?

We offer a 7-day satisfaction guarantee. If the print quality doesn't match the design preview, we'll reprint it or issue a full refund — no hassle.
